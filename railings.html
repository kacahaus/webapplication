KACA HAUS Railings · Frameless Glass Systems

Frameless & glass railing systems, engineered and installed in-house.

KACA HAUS Railings is our in-house glass railing line — base-shoe, standoff/spigot and stainless steel handrail systems in toughened and laminated safety glass. Every system is specified to the opening's height, wind exposure and live-load requirement, then fabricated and installed by our own crews for a single point of accountability from drawing to handover.
