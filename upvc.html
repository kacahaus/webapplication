Encraft & Gluten · UPVC Systems

Precision UPVC fenestration, engineered for Hyderabad's climate.

Encraft and Gluten are our authorized UPVC partners, offering multi-chambered casement, tilt-and-turn and sliding window & door systems with strong acoustic and thermal performance. Encraft brings reinforced, multi-point-locking profiles for high-rise and villa openings, while Gluten delivers durable, energy-efficient profiles with a wide range of finishes. KACAHAUS supplies, fabricates and installs the complete range.
